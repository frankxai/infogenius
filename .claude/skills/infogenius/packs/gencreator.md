# Style pack: gencreator

Source: `frankxai/starlight-design-intelligence@851a11b`, `brand-image-system/runtime/brands/gencreator/brand-pack.json`. Territory B (new creative publishing), approved 2026-08-22. If that file changes, regenerate this pack; do not edit colors here by hand.

## Use when
The visual is for a creator, a newsletter edition or a GenCreator surface: the default pack for carousels and essay explainers.

## Colors
- Paper `#fbf9f5` (background)
- Ink `#221f1b` (text and line)
- Signal red `#c8322a`: once per object, to mark edition, source, state or signature

Paper and ink carry at least 90% of the composition. Spot red registers edition, source, state or signature once per object. Brand color alone must not communicate state: pair it with a label.

## Direction
- Make authorship and source evidence visible: cite the source on the visual.
- Treat each visual as an authored edition (number, date or source line).
- Transform source fragments through crop, scale or sequence.

## Never
- The legacy blue/cyan/green command-centre look
- Generated wordmarks or symbols presented as masters
- A generic creator desk, faceless automated volume, decorative 3D logo renders
- Phrases: "10x your content", "effortless growth", "creator revolution"

## Prompt lines to add
```text
PALETTE: warm paper #fbf9f5 background, ink #221f1b type and linework, a single signal red #c8322a mark on the source or edition label.
COMPOSITION: editorial print layout; paper and ink carry 90% of the image; cite the source in a small caption.
```
